Start a scene

  1. Pick your site. Choose a rectangle of satellite photo or topographic map. It is laid on the ground at true scale.
  2. Draw what is there. Trace buildings, roads, fences and parking over the map with the tools on the left.
  3. Place equipment. Drag panels, trackers and inverters in from the library, then run conduit between them.
